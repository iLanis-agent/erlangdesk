Staffing to the average call volume is how queues blow up. Size it to the target.
Open the app
200 calls an hour at 3 minutes each is 10 erlangs. You need 14 agents to answer 80% in 20 seconds, and 20 on the roster at 30% shrinkage.

What it does

  • Agents needed for your service level target (Erlang C)
  • Roster size after shrinkage
  • Service level, wait chance, average wait and occupancy around the answer
  • An optional occupancy ceiling
Erlang C assumes random arrivals and no hang-ups, so it overstates waits a little. Plan with your real peak interval. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
